# Russian Checkers (Русские шашки) Official Rules

## Board & Setup
- Played on an 8×8 board using only the 32 dark squares.
- The board is oriented so that the bottom-left corner square is dark (a1).
- Each player starts with 12 pieces (*шашки*), arranged on the three rows closest to them:
  - White on rows 1 to 3 (indices 5, 6, 7)
  - Black on rows 6 to 8 (indices 0, 1, 2)
- White moves first.

## Movement Rules
1. **Simple Piece (Простая шашка)**:
   - Moves diagonally forward one square to an adjacent unoccupied dark square.
2. **Flying King (Дамка)**:
   - When a piece reaches the opposite back row (promotion row), it is crowned a King (*дамка*).
   - A King can move any number of empty squares diagonally in any of the four directions (flying king).

## Capture Rules (Бой)
1. **Mandatory Capture**:
   - If a capture is available, the player **must** capture.
   - Unlike international draughts, there is no requirement to choose the path capturing the largest number of pieces. Any valid capture chain is legal.
2. **Backward Capture**:
   - Simple pieces can capture both **forward and backward**.
3. **In-Flight Crown Promotion**:
   - If a simple piece reaches the king row during a capture sequence and can continue jumping from that square as a king, it is crowned immediately and continues the capture sequence according to king rules!
4. **Captured Pieces Removal**:
   - Captured pieces are removed from the board only after the complete capture jump sequence is completed (the "Turkish strike" rule: a piece cannot be jumped twice during the same turn).

## Win & Draw Conditions
- A player **wins** if their opponent has no legal moves remaining (all pieces captured or blocked).
- A **draw** occurs upon mutual agreement, threefold repetition of the same board state, or if both players have kings and the 15-move rule is reached without changes in piece count.
- **Giveaway mode (Поддавки)**: The winner is the player who loses all their pieces or is unable to move.
